Medication tracker

A simple daily record of how you are doing on medication. It stays on your device, and you can export it as a spreadsheet to bring to your reviews.

Please read: this is a personal record to share with your doctor, not medical advice. It is not a reason to change, start, or stop your medication on your own. Always follow your doctor's guidance on dosing. Nothing you enter here leaves your device.

Adverse effects

For example: feeling jittery or wired, dizziness, sweating, a change in weight, stomach upset, drowsiness, or feeling low or irritable. Note anything you want to raise.

If you ever have chest pain, a racing or irregular heartbeat that does not settle, fainting, a marked change in mood, or any thoughts of harming yourself, do not just note it here. Contact your doctor, or your nearest hospital if it is urgent.
